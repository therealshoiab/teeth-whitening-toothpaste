Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about our peroxide-free whitening science and daily oral routine.

How quickly will I notice whiter teeth? +

Most customers notice an immediate 1 to 2 shade brightening after their first use due to our instant purple color-correction technology. Completing the full 7-day treatment delivers up to 4 shades whiter teeth with long-lasting results.

Will Perfora cause any tooth or gum sensitivity? +

Zero sensitivity guaranteed! Traditional whitening products use harsh hydrogen peroxide that dehydrates teeth and burns dentin. Perfora uses biocompatible Hydroxyapatite (n-Ha) and PAP to actively strengthen enamel while lifting organic surface stains.

How do I use the Purple Whitening Strips? +

Dry your teeth with a tissue, peel the universal strip from its backing, and press it firmly onto your upper or lower teeth. Keep it on for 30 minutes. The strip naturally dissolves on its own with a clean minty freshness—no sticky residue or rinsing required!

Is it safe for crowns, veneers, caps, and fillings? +

Yes. Our non-abrasive, SLS-free formulations are 100% safe for artificial dental work. While it will not bleach crowns past their initial manufactured shade, it safely lifts accumulated tea, coffee, and food stains to restore their original brightness.

What are your shipping times and delivery charges? +

We provide Free Express Delivery across India on all orders. Orders are processed within 24 hours, and standard transit takes 2-4 business days for metro locations and 3-5 days for other regions. Cash on Delivery (COD) and instant UPI are both supported.
